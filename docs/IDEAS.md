Ideas raised while planning the take-home. The first is being built; the rest are logged to discuss and possibly build after.

## Building now

### 1. The coach that remembers
Review one game in the context of the player's recent games. The coach reports recurring themes across wins, losses and mistakes, and in a single-game review says how each key moment fits the pattern.
- Origin: Atreyu, session 01.
- Why first: it is the gap between Chess.com's single-game review and its statistics page.

## Next

### 2. Puzzles from your own blunders
For a problem area the coach found, drill the player on their own positions. The engine's move is the answer, so nothing is invented.
- Origin: agent suggestion, session 01; Atreyu wants it as the first follow-on feature.
- Depends on: the fact table from idea 1 (position before the mistake, engine's move).

### 3. Scout an opponent
Study one opponent's games and prepare for their style: the openings they play, where they go wrong, what to play against them.
- Origin: agent suggestion, session 01. Atreyu's use for it: they have never beaten their dad (about 1500) and want to.
- Depends on: the same fetch, engine and fact layers, pointed at someone else's games.
- Open question: what "prepare" can honestly claim. Opening frequency and results are provable; "play this line against him" needs the engine or an opening book behind it.
